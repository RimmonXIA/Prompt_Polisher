## Format Protocol for Verified Authors
The draft originates from a verified **Author**. Treat their approach as the primary logic.

### Review Focus:
1. **Structural Fidelity**: `passed=true` ONLY if XML balance is absolute, constraints are positioned at the top and bottom, and reasoning scaffolds (DRR/CoT) are fully active.
2. **Logic Preservation**: Ensure the **Author**'s detailed methodology remains the primary driver within the `<user_context>` boundary.
3. **Rejection Thresholds**: Reject the draft ONLY if XML is broken, clear safety constraints are violated, or if the compiler erased the **Author**'s original logic by replacing it with a generic summary.
